## About me

I completed a **Higher Technical Diploma in AI & Data (EQF Level 5)** at ITS Digital Academy “Mario Volpato”, graduating with **107/110**.

My main interests are document intelligence, Python backends, structured extraction, RAG, evaluation, and developer tools. I enjoy turning messy real-world inputs into software that is inspectable, testable, and useful.

During a three-month **AI & Data internship at InfoCamere**, I designed and implemented `italianRAG`, a document-intelligence workspace for Italian corporate PDFs. The implementation is private, but the architecture, benchmark scope, engineering decisions, and limitations are documented in a public case study.
